# Hishab privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Hishab ("the app") is a personal money tracker. This page says what the app does
with your information.

## What stays on your phone
Everything you write in Hishab (accounts, entries, categories, budgets, loans,
goals, notes, settings) is stored only on your phone, in a database that other
apps cannot read. The developer does not receive it, cannot see it and does not
keep a copy. There is no account to create, no advertising and no analytics or
tracking of any kind. Removing the app removes this data from your phone.

## Information that leaves your phone
The app uses the internet only for the following, and sends no personal data and
no data from your records:
- **Exchange rate and gold and silver prices**: when you ask for them, the app
  requests today's dollar rate and gold and silver prices from public services
  (open.er-api.com, api.gold-api.com, and cdn.jsdelivr.net as a fallback). Like
  any request on the internet, it reveals your IP address to those services.
- **Google Drive backup (optional)**: if you choose to back up to Google Drive,
  you sign in with your Google account and allow the app to keep its own files
  (the permission "drive.file"). The app then writes one backup file,
  "Hishab-backup.json", to your own Google Drive, and reads it back when you
  restore. The file goes straight between your phone and your Drive. The
  developer cannot see it. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google
  account and your Drive. You can sign out in the app, delete the file in Drive,
  or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Files you choose to make or share
Backups, CSV exports and reports are files you create and decide where to keep or
send (for example with the share sheet). The app does not send them anywhere
else. Reading an SMS: you paste the text of a message into the app yourself; the
app does not read your messages.

## Permissions
- Internet: for the items above.
- Notifications: reminders you set (bills, loans, recurring items).
- Receive boot completed and vibrate: so reminders return after the phone
  restarts and can vibrate.

## Children
Hishab is not directed at children.

## Your choices and deleting your data
All your data is on your phone: delete entries in the app, or clear the app's
data or uninstall it in Android settings. A backup in your own Google Drive is
yours to delete there.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address
with a new date.
