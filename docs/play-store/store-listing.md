## English
**App name** (22/30): Hishab - Money Tracker

**Short description** (80/80):
Track income, expenses, loans and budgets. Works offline, in Bangla and English.

**Full description** (1657/4000):

Hishab keeps your money in order, on your phone, without an account and without internet.

WRITE DOWN MONEY IN SECONDS
- Add an expense, income or transfer with a calculator keypad, or add several at once.
- Paste a bKash, bank or card SMS and Hishab fills in the amount, the account and the type.
- Cash, bKash, Nagad, bank accounts and credit cards (with limit, bill day and dollar spending).
- Categories inside categories, made for daily life in Bangladesh: Kacha bazar, rickshaw, house rent, tuition, Salami and more.

SEE WHERE IT GOES
- A Home screen with what is safe to spend, your wallets and recent entries.
- Monthly budgets, with a pace line that shows where the month is heading.
- Reports by category, account, week and month, and a comparison with the month before.
- A short "Month wrapped" story, spending insights and streaks.

DHAR AND BAKI
- Loans you gave and loans you took, with due dates, part payments and reminders.
- Add a late fee or interest to a loan you took, and the transfer charge when you pay it back.
- Set off what two people owe each other, and split a bill with friends.

PLAN AHEAD
- Savings goals, including a fund for Eid.
- Recurring bills and salary, entered once.
- A bazar list that remembers prices.
- Trips and events, gold and silver valued today, business investments and a zakat calculator.

YOURS ALONE
- Works without internet. Your data stays on your phone.
- No ads, no tracking, no account.
- Back up to a file, share it, or keep it in your own Google Drive. Import and export CSV.
- Full Bangla and English, light and dark.

Hishab is made for people who want a simple, honest record of their money.
