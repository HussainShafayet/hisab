# Why Hishab is different

Plenty of money apps exist. Hishab is built for one person's money in
Bangladesh, and each point below is something the app does that a general app
does not (or does only after much setup).

1. **It reads your bKash / Nagad / bank SMS** (paste it, one tap): amount, fee,
   account, day, TrxID, a transfer for a cash out with its fee, the same SMS
   never twice, and a balance check that shows when an entry is missing. No SMS
   permission: you choose what it sees. [Paste an SMS](../transactions/sms-paste.md)
2. **Written the way people spend**: one line (`rickshaw 50 bkash`,
   `bazar 450 gotokal`), earlier notes offered again with their category and
   amount, categories for kacha bazar, salami, fitra, tuition.
   [Quick type](../transactions/quick-type.md), [Note suggestions](../transactions/note-suggestions.md)
3. **Money between people**: dhar and baki with reminders to send, a split of a
   bill with friends. [Loans](../debts/loans.md)
4. **The year as it is lived here**: Eid, Qurbani and Pohela Boishakh funds
   with a countdown, zakat in bhori and ana with the live gold price.
5. **Private on a shared phone**: hide balances, hide income with a bar only the
   owner can read, no account, no internet needed, nothing sent anywhere.
   [Hide income](../settings/hide-income.md)
6. **Bangla, all the way**: every screen, the digits, the guide.
7. **Hard to lose your work**: it asks before a typed form is thrown away, an
   import can be undone, and a backup reminder comes on Home.
   [Leave without saving](leave-without-saving.md)

The test for a new feature: does it make writing down, or understanding, the
money of a Bangladeshi household easier? If not, it should not be here.
